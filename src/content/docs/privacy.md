---
title: "Privacy Policy"
description: "Moe Memos collects no data and makes no network connections to the developer."
lang: "en"
---

Moe Memos is built with full respect for user privacy in mind. **Moe Memos does not collect any data and makes no network connections to the developer.**

You can use Moe Memos entirely locally on your device, where all memos and attachments remain stored on your hardware.

Moe Memos can also connect to a self-hosted [Memos](https://github.com/usememos/memos) server to sync your data across devices. When connected, Moe Memos only stores your Access Token in your device's secure storage and communicates directly with the server you configure.

The developer of Moe Memos strongly recommends hosting your Memos server on your own local hardware to fully protect your data. If you prefer to host your Memos server on the internet (a.k.a. someone else's computer), you are subject to the privacy policy of your hosting provider. Please ensure that you connect to your Memos server only via HTTPS or a trusted local network.

*Moe Memos is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with the open-source project [Memos](https://github.com/usememos/memos).*
